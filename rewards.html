GOOD TO KNOW

Rewards FAQ

Everything you need to know about this demo program.

How do I earn points? +

In this demo, click the "Add 10 Points" button. A real rewards program would connect points to purchases.

How many points do I need? +

You need 30 points for a cookie, 60 points for a brownie, or 100 points for a cappuccino.

Will my points stay after refresh? +

Yes, if your browser allows local storage. Points are saved only in the browser you're using.

Can I use these rewards in the cafe? +

No. This is a front-end project demonstration. Demo points cannot be exchanged for real products.
